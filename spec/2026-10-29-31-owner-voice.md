# Spec: only the owner's voice is labelled as the owner

Inputs: the approved intent and its decisions (kept as "Room", explicit
sample, on once a sample exists with a pause switch, built after rewrite #3
step 10); read-only research by Codex (gpt-6-sol) and agy (model not
reported) on 2026-10-29, checked against the code and the linked library by
the lead.

## Design

### 1. The model: CAM++ through the linked sherpa-onnx

- `3dspeaker_speech_campplus_sv_zh_en_16k-common_advanced.onnx` (26 MB,
  16 kHz) from the sherpa-onnx `speaker-recongition-models` release.
  Weights Apache-2.0 (ModelScope `iic/speech_campplus_sv_zh_en_16k-common_advanced`,
  checked 2026-10-29), trained on a large Chinese-English set, not
  VoxCeleb-only, so no dataset-terms question.
- New `nix/speaker-model.nix` fetches it with a fixed hash, as
  `nix/parakeet-model.nix` does for Parakeet; the Home Manager module puts
  its path in the daemon's environment. The app never downloads it.
- Called through `sherpa_onnx::SpeakerEmbeddingExtractor` (present in the
  linked 1.13.8 library: `SherpaOnnxCreateSpeakerEmbeddingExtractor`
  checked). Loaded once, lazily, on the first check; not loaded at all
  without a sample.

### 2. The voice sample: an embedding, never audio

- Daemon commands (socket only, refused for `--agent` and MCP callers):
  - `voice.enroll`: records 12 s from the current mic source while no
    meeting records, while the overlay shows a sentence to read. Needs at
    least 8 s of speech (level gate on 1.5 s windows); else replies with
    the reason and stores nothing.
  - `voice.status`: `{enrolled, model, created, paused}`. Never the vector.
  - `voice.delete`: removes the sample.
- Stored: the averaged, normalised embedding of the speech windows (192
  floats), the model file name and the time, in a new table `voice_sample`
  (one row) in the SQLCipher database. The recorded audio lives only in
  memory during enrollment and is dropped after.
- A sample made with another model file is treated as absent and the
  overlay asks for a new one.

### 3. The check: per mic line, after transcription

- `src/speech/session/listen.rs` keeps the last 30 s of 16 kHz mic samples
  in a fixed ring buffer (allocated once at start), fed at the same point
  as the existing echo gate, after mute.
- When a final mic line arrives (from Parakeet or Deepgram alike, it
  carries `start` and `end`), the daemon cuts that span from the ring,
  embeds it on a blocking thread and compares it with the sample (cosine):
  - span shorter than 1.5 s of speech, or score between the two
    thresholds: stays the owner's ("unclear stays mine");
  - score under the low threshold: labelled `room`.
  The thresholds come from the measurement in Verification and are fixed
  in code with that evidence in a comment.
- Engine-agnostic: works on audio, not text, so both engines get it with
  one code path.
- Cost: one embedding per mic line (lines are seconds long), on its own
  thread; the line is shown at once as the owner's and relabelled within
  about a second if the check says `room` (the overlay updates the row).

### 4. "Room" through the app

- DB `who` gains `room` next to `me` and `them`; old rows are unchanged.
- Overlay (`shell/Transcript.qml`): label "Room", same style as "Them",
  not "You".
- Recaps, notes and live answers (`src/ai/recap.rs` and prompt building)
  render `room` lines as "Someone in the room", never as the owner, and
  never give them the owner's action items.
- Settings: `voice_check_paused` (bool, default false). The overlay's
  settings show the state, "Record my voice", "Record again", "Delete".

### 5. Order

Implemented after rewrite #3 step 10 merges, on the rewritten `listen.rs`
and session files (intent decision 4).

## Alternatives rejected

- **Alkove Timbre** (0.74 MB): a second inference engine from an
  unpublished preview crate; CAM++ comes through the library already linked.
- **NeMo TitaNet** (CC-BY-4.0): trained partly on VoxCeleb; larger.
- **Checking fixed audio windows instead of lines:** lines are what get
  labelled; windows would need a second mapping to lines.
- **Dropping non-owner lines:** decided against in the intent; words would
  be lost.
- **Storing the enrollment audio:** more biometric data than needed; the
  embedding is enough, and a new model means a new sample anyway.

## Risks

- **Wrong "Room" on the owner** (cold, whisper, other microphone): limited
  by "unclear stays mine" and thresholds set from the owner's own
  recordings; the pause switch is one click. Measured before merge.
- **Mixed lines** (someone speaks over the owner): stay the owner's by
  design; this check does not separate voices (that is #32's question).
- **Line times vs audio times:** Deepgram's and Parakeet's line times must
  map to the ring's sample offsets; tested with a fixture where the
  mapping is known.
- **Model size:** 26 MB more in the closure on every host with the module.
- **Rewrite #3:** waiting for step 10 delays this; building it first would
  make the clean-room step rewrite it again.

## Verification

- Unit tests (no models, no devices): cosine and threshold decisions,
  "unclear stays mine", ring-buffer cut by time across the wrap, `room`
  rendering in recaps and prompts, enrollment refusing too little speech,
  `voice.*` refused for agent callers, sample with another model treated as
  absent.
- Integration test with the real model, as `parakeet-decode` does in the
  flake checks: generated speech of two different voices; the second is
  labelled `room` against a sample of the first.
- Measurement on p620 with the #14 evaluation tools (recordings of the
  owner made for #32, plus test speech from a phone): foreign lines
  labelled as the owner and owner lines labelled `room`, check off vs on.
  Merge only if owner lines lost to `room` are at most 1 in 50.
- `cargo fmt`, `clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace`, the shell's
  headless tests, `nix flake check`.
