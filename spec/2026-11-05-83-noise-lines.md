# Spec: Noise does not become transcript lines

## Design

**Measure first, and change code only if the check wins.** This is a measurement against a written rule, on built sets, using `examples/accuracy_replay.rs` and `tools/accuracy/`. Nothing in the live path changes until a result exists. The owner decided doing nothing is acceptable, and that no real-call recording is needed, so the result states that limit.

### The test sets

| Set | Source | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Noise | #73's bursts, room tone and silence, samples 1 and 2 (`~/.cache/meetingai-eval/en73/`) | Invented lines |
| **Real short utterances (new)** | AMI meetings already cached for #61 (`~/.cache/meetingai-eval/ami-hf-ihm`): utterances whose human transcript has at most 3 words, such as "yeah", "okay", "mm-hmm" and "no", at most 1.5 s of speech | Utterances kept |
| Guards | #73's `libri`, `double_talk` and `remote_only` | Word errors, bleed, delay |

The new set is what #73 lacked: real meeting speech with a human transcript. These are short utterances, not proven replies, since the transcript doesn't show who they answer. That's enough here, because the check judges each chunk on its own. Each is played through the batch chunker, so it's judged as a chunk would be in a call. It's split by meeting, half for tuning and half held back for the final number. A new builder, `tools/accuracy/short_replies.py`, makes it from the cache. AMI's audio is close-talk meeting audio, not a compressed call, and the result says so.

### What is compared

1. **Today's build,** as the baseline.
2. **A voice-activity check before decoding (Silero).** Run the model on a finished chunk, and don't send a chunk to Parakeet if it finds less than a set amount of speech. sherpa-onnx 2.0.1, already linked, has this (`src/vad.rs`). The model is about 2 MB and was in the closure until #77 (`nix/silero-vad.nix`). Tuned on the tuning half for the threshold and minimum speech.

Confidence scores aren't compared, since the batch result is plain text.

### The rule that decides

The check wins only if **all** of these hold on the held-back half and both noise samples. The baseline is today's build re-measured on the same sets, since #73 reports noise for sample 1 only.

- **Noise:** invented lines on bursts fall by at least 75% from each sample's own baseline, and room tone doesn't rise.
- **Real utterances:** at least as many kept as today, counted per utterance, and every newly lost one is listed. This is the intent's "at least as well": no allowance.
- **Word errors and bleed:** word errors within 0.5 points of the re-measured baseline, and no more bleed lines.
- **No added delay:** median and p95 per channel no worse than the spread between two baseline runs of the same set. This allows for measurement noise, not for slower lines.
- **Cost:** the check's own time, timed for every chunk including those it rejects, under 10 ms at the median. Fewer requests to Parakeet can't hide it, because it's timed separately.

If it doesn't win, **nothing changes**: the result is written to `docs/eval/`, and #83 is closed with that finding.

### If one wins

The plan's measuring part ends there and comes back to the owner with the numbers. The code part is planned only then, and it's approved separately. It would change `src/speech/stt/transcription.rs` near `MIN_SOUND_SAMPLES`, plus `nix/` for the model in the closure, with no setting. Streaming Deepgram isn't affected. The replay tool gains one flag for the check in the measuring part.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Blocking words.** "Yeah" and "mm" are real replies.
- **Dropping short lines with no speech just before them.** The noise sets have no conversation, so it would score 100% there without showing anything, and it can't catch noise during a conversation. It would also drop real openings such as "Hello?" and replies after a pause. `LineFilter` sees accepted lines rather than speech, holds mic lines for 3 s, and a line's length includes 0.6 s of closing quiet, so it wouldn't even work as described.
- **Raising `MIN_SOUND_SAMPLES` or `SOUND_LEVEL`.** They count loud samples, and a cough is loud. It would lose quiet real replies first.
- **A user setting.** Nobody can tune this during a call, and it would avoid the decision.
- **Recording real noise.** The owner chose built sets. The result states this limit, and real recordings can be added later.
- **A new speech model.** #77 settled on Parakeet.

## Risks

- **AMI isn't the owner's calls.** Its speakers, microphones and accents differ, and a pass doesn't prove real calls are fine. A win still gets a check by the owner on a real meeting.
- **MUSAN bursts are a harsh test,** so a large fall there may overstate the real gain.
- **Tuning to the set.** The held-back half guards against this, and it's used once.
- **Noise during a conversation isn't measured,** since no set mixes the two. A check that judges each chunk alone should behave the same there, but the result states this.
- **The rule is strict and may reject a useful check.** That's intended: the owner chose losing an occasional "Uh" over losing a reply.
- **A voice-activity model can miss short or quiet speech.** That's exactly what the new set measures, and it may rule out option 2.
- **Load on p620.** Measuring uses natural load, one run at a time, after checking the agent bus and other sessions' CI. No artificial load.

## Verification

- The builder has an assert-based self-check: every clip's reference has at most 3 words and is at most 1.5 s, the two halves share no meeting, and a fixed seed makes it repeatable.
- `tools/accuracy/selfcheck.py` still passes, and it gains a check for the new set's scoring.
- Baseline repeated twice on today's build. It should match #73's numbers for sample 1 before anything is compared, and the two runs give the delay spread.
- `docs/eval/2026-11-noise-lines.md` records the sets, each approach's numbers against the rule, the limits, and the decision.
- `cargo test --workspace`, clippy and `cargo fmt --check` for the replay flags and any builder.
