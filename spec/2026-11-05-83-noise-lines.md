# Spec: Noise does not become transcript lines

## Design

**Measure first, change code only if an approach wins.** This is a measurement against a written rule, on built sets, using `examples/accuracy_replay.rs` and `tools/accuracy/`. Nothing in the live path changes until a result exists. The owner decided doing nothing is acceptable, and that no real-call recording is needed, so the result states that limit.

### The test sets

| Set | Source | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Noise | #73's bursts, room tone and silence, samples 1 and 2 (`~/.cache/meetingai-eval/en73/`) | Invented lines |
| **Real short replies (new)** | AMI meetings already cached for #61 (`~/.cache/meetingai-eval/ami-hf-ihm`): utterances whose reference has at most 3 words, such as "yeah", "okay", "mm-hmm" and "no", at most 1.5 s long | Replies kept |
| Short replies in place | The same replies with up to 10 s of their real meeting audio around them, so a neighbour rule can be tested | Replies kept when neighbours matter |
| Guards | #73's `libri`, `double_talk` and `remote_only` | Word errors, bleed, delay |

The new set is what #73 lacked. It's real meeting speech with a human transcript, so it can show whether a change loses real replies. It's split in two, half for tuning and half held back for the final number. A new builder, `tools/accuracy/short_replies.py`, makes it from the cache. AMI's audio is close-talk meeting audio, not a compressed call, and the result says so.

### What is compared

1. **Today's build,** as the baseline.
2. **A voice-activity check before decoding (Silero).** Run the model on a finished chunk, and don't send a chunk to Parakeet if it finds less than a set amount of speech. sherpa-onnx 2.0.1, already linked, has this (`src/vad.rs`). The model is about 2 MB and was in the closure until #77 (`nix/silero-vad.nix`). Tuned on the tuning half for the threshold and minimum speech.
3. **An isolation rule on lines.** In `LineFilter`, drop a line of at most 2 words, under about 1.2 s, when neither channel had speech just before it. Using only the past adds no delay. It relies on a real reply normally following someone's speech. It can't help noise that happens during a conversation, and the measurement shows how much that matters.

Confidence scores aren't compared, since the batch result is plain text.

### The rule that decides

An approach wins only if, on both noise samples and the held-back half, **all** of these hold:

- **Noise:** invented lines on bursts fall by at least 75%, from 16 to at most 4 on sample 1, and room tone doesn't rise.
- **Real replies:** no more than 1% fewer kept than today, counted per reply, since losing one is worse than a stray "Uh".
- **Guards:** word errors within 0.5 points of #73's baselines, bleed lines no more than the baseline (7 double talk, 6 remote only), median and p95 delay no more than 50 ms worse per channel.
- **Cost:** a chunk's added processing time under 10 ms at the median.

If both win, take the smaller change. If neither wins, **nothing changes**: the result is written to `docs/eval/`, and #83 is closed with that finding.

### If one wins

The plan's measuring part ends there and comes back to the owner with the numbers. The code part is planned only then, since its shape depends on the winner, and it's approved separately. It would change `src/speech/stt/` only, with no setting: `transcription.rs` near `MIN_SOUND_SAMPLES` for the check, or `filter.rs` for the rule. For the check, also `nix/` and the Parakeet option's closure. Streaming Deepgram isn't affected, and the replay tool gains one flag for each approach, in the measuring part.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Blocking words.** "Yeah" and "mm" are real replies.
- **Raising `MIN_SOUND_SAMPLES` or `SOUND_LEVEL`.** They count loud samples, and a cough is loud. It would lose quiet real replies first.
- **A user setting.** Nobody can tune this during a call, and it would avoid the decision.
- **Recording real noise.** The owner chose built sets. The result states this limit, and real recordings can be added later.
- **A new speech model.** #77 settled on Parakeet.

## Risks

- **AMI isn't the owner's calls.** Its speakers, microphones and accents differ, and a pass doesn't prove real calls are fine. A win still gets a check by the owner on a real meeting.
- **MUSAN bursts are a harsh test,** so a large fall there may overstate the real gain.
- **Tuning to the set.** The held-back half guards against this, and it's used once.
- **A voice-activity model can miss short or quiet speech.** That's exactly what the new set measures, and it may rule out option 2.
- **Load on p620.** Measuring uses natural load, one run at a time, after checking the agent bus and other sessions' CI. No artificial load.

## Verification

- The builder has an assert-based self-check: every clip's reference has at most 3 words and is at most 1.5 s, the two halves share no meeting, and a fixed seed makes it repeatable.
- `tools/accuracy/selfcheck.py` still passes, and it gains a check for the new set's scoring.
- Baseline repeated on today's build, matching #73's noise and guard numbers before anything is compared.
- `docs/eval/2026-11-noise-lines.md` records the sets, each approach's numbers against the rule, the limits, and the decision.
- `cargo test --workspace`, clippy and `cargo fmt --check` for the replay flags and any builder.
